PLAN: full closed-loop test of the stop decision in the simulated orchard   (written 16 Nov 2026, before any run with seed 2026)

Why: closed_loop_sim.py replays recorded drives (camera view, path and speed are fixed). Here (row_stop_sim.py, PyChrono 10)
the robot really drives on soft soil, slips, brakes, and every check is made from where the robot actually is.
The VLM sees the simulated camera image live and its real answer time is part of the loop.

WORLD (frozen = row_stop_sim.py as committed with this plan; tuned on seed 0 during setup, see "known limits"):
  8 parallel hedge rows of sphere canopies (radius 1.5-1.9 m, overlapping), clear lane 3.2-4.0 m, robot = Jackal-size
  skid-steer, camera like the CitrusFarm ZED2i (0.28 m above ground, 102 deg), soils firm / soft / mud in turn.
  Scenario row: open headland, then the row.  Scenario lookalike: first a wide corridor between two short hedges
  (canopy faces 3.5-5 m from the robot, x = -14..-8 m), then the open headland, then the row.
  Entrance label = farm labelling rule (canopy of the two lane rows reaches both image edges), from the geometry.
  Success = robot at rest within 2 m of the entrance. Early = stopped >= 2 m before it.
DECIDERS AND POLICIES (unchanged from the farm work):
  depth = frozen farm rule (edge bands, both < 3.5 m).  vlm = probe_arrival.py question, temperature 0, seed 42, blank = NO.
  drive = decide while driving, stop after 3 YES in a row (VLM: the robot keeps driving during each answer).
  step6 = move-stop-ask: 1 m steps, 0.25 m after the first YES, stop at 6 YES in a row (robot stands while asking).
RUNS (all with --seed 2026, never used before):
  E1 depth, ray casting (CPU): {row, lookalike} x {drive, step6}, 30 trials each.
  E2 depth, depth camera (GPU): {row, lookalike} x {drive, step6}, 10 trials each (checks E1 with real rendering).
  E3 VLM live: qwen3-vl:4b with wording A (main) and qwen3-vl:8b-instruct with wording C (second),
     {row, lookalike} x {step6, drive}, 10 trials each.
  Allowed before E3: one technical check that the query images are upright and show the scene (looked at, not scored).
REPORT: successes, early / late / no stop, mean error, mission time (incl. thinking time), per scenario, policy, decider.
QUESTIONS STATED IN ADVANCE:
  Q1 Does depth + drive stop well in both scenarios (>= 24/30 each)?
  Q2 Does the lookalike corridor cause more early stops for the VLM than the plain row (step6)?
  Q3 Is VLM + drive worse than VLM + step6 (thinking time costs metres when driving)?
  Q4 Is depth + drive better than the best VLM setting in the lookalike scenario?
KNOWN LIMITS (stated now): the world (canopy shape, rows, camera) was adjusted while debugging with seed 0;
  in the simulator the edge depth falls below 3.5 m ~2 m later relative to the label than on the farm
  (farm_vs_sim_depth.png), so step6 tends to stop late here; canopies are smooth spheres (rendered images look simpler
  than the farm, a domain gap for the VLM); one robot speed (1.2 m/s); straight approach, no turn into the row.
NOT ALLOWED after results: changing the world, the rule, K, D, the label, the seed, the number of trials or the models.
