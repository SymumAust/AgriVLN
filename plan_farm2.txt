("the trees on both sides are behind you"; "the block is right beside you" when it is leaving the view).

WORDING RULE (fixed from now on, for every new episode):
  1. The arrival condition describes what the camera SEES at the stop, never what is behind or out of view.
  2. It names a positive visible state: an object and where it is in the image (centre, left edge, fills the lower half),
     or the scene layout (trees close on both sides, open ground ahead).
  3. Keep distinctive attributes (colour, material, "nearest").
  4. Check it on the stop frame (true) and on a frame about 5 s earlier (false) before running anything.
  5. Grammar "stop when <thing> is/are <state>" so probe_arrival.py can split it.
