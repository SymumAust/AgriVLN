PLAN: Field step 4 - where should the camera be mounted? (depth v3 only)   (written 26 Nov 2026, before any run)

Why: in steps 2-3 the 0.28 m camera saw the ditch so late that the robot stopped 0.00-0.01 m from the edge and once touched
it. Before the site visit (30 Nov) we want to tell the professor which camera height and tilt the stereo camera needs.

SETUP: FieldWorld.cs (camera height/tilt now set by Python on every reset), depth_v3.py (now tilt-aware: points are rotated
into the robot frame; with tilt 0 the result is unchanged - offline check on run_field_drive still gives 92.9 %),
field_client.py --policy depth_v3. Everything else as in step 2: thresholds 0.15 / 0.05 m, zones from the current speed,
obstacle at z 30 m, x 0, start 18-22 m before, seed 2026, depth grid 64 x 36.
CONFIGS (cam_sweep.sh): height 0.28 / 0.6 / 1.0 m with tilt 0, and 0.6 / 1.0 m with tilt 15 deg down.
  8 scenarios x 3 trials each. Summary: cam_summary.py.

GEOMETRY BEFORE RUNNING (computed, not measured): with tilt 0 the camera first sees the ground 0.41 m (h 0.28), 0.89 m (h 0.6)
and 1.48 m (h 1.0) ahead; with tilt 15: 0.52 m (h 0.6), 0.87 m (h 1.0). A 1 m ditch can only be found when ground rays are
less than ~1 m apart there: with this grid up to about sqrt(h / 0.034) m ahead = 2.9 m (h 0.28), 4.2 m (h 0.6), 5.4 m (h 1.0).
depth v3 has NO memory: what leaves the view is forgotten.

  P2  h 0.6 (t 0 and t 15): ditch first slow at 3.8 m or more, final gap 0.3 m or more, no collision in any scenario.
