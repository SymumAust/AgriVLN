PLAN: Field step 5 (= step A) - depth decider v4 with memory   (written 28 Nov 2026, before any Unity run of v4)

Why: step 4 showed that the depth decider forgets an obstacle or ditch once it slips into the camera's near blind zone,
that a detour decided at d_hazard is too late (side out of view, no room to swerve), and that the hole distance is
over-estimated. v4 (depth_v4.py) adds:
  1. memory: a 0.1 m local grid in world coordinates from the robot pose (Unity pose = perfect odometry - a limitation);
     ground seen later clears old marks; what is out of view keeps its last mark; cells > 15 m away are forgotten;
  2. early detour: as soon as an obstacle is in the warning zone, the free strip beside it is measured in the map;
     >= 0.73 m -> DETOUR at once;
  3. careful hole distance: a hole is marked at the ground point of the next closer ray (the near edge is between them).
  Thresholds, corridor and zones unchanged (0.15 / 0.05 m, 0.365 m half corridor, t_r 0.3, a 1.0, d_m 0.5, t_w 3).
  Offline check before writing this plan (step-1 drive recording, constant 1 m/s; done to catch bugs): v4 slowed for the ditch
  at a true 4.8 m and stopped at a true 1.4 m; rock/drum/people: detour at a true 3.1 m; empty path: never left go.
